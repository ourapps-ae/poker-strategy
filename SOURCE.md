Reference revision: `19905b75dbea89fbfd424d67fe26c81aaed0e018`. Extraction source: the `recipe/` folder of `ourapps-ae/OurPoker`; paths below are relative to it.

- `implementation/server/strategy.ts` <- `server/src/bot.ts` lines 81-192 (`botAction`, `assumedRange`, `dist`, `average`, `aggression`, `observe`, `learnHand`, `OpponentStats`, `BotMemory`).
  - Generalised: `botAction(game, bot, rng, memory)` read the engine's `Game` and `Player` and the view's `legalFor`; `decide(input, rng, memory)` takes a plain `DecisionInput` (cards, board, street, stack, bets, seat, button, blinds, bet to match, pot, legal actions, live opponents with their last action) so any rules component can feed it. The name-keyed personality table (`PERSONALITIES`, `personality`) is not here: a `Style` {tight, aggr, bluff} is passed per call and the default `aggr` comes from the `aggression` setting (0-100). Seats is a parameter instead of the constant 9. `observe` takes the street instead of a `Game`; `learnHand` takes player ids. Equity and Chen's score come from poker-odds (`winChance`, `startingScore`) instead of being defined here.
  - New: `difficulty` 1 to 3. Difficulty 2 is the recipe's exact numbers (200 pre-flop and 300 post-flop trials, noise 0.08, opponent ranges and reads on). Difficulty 1 samples 80 and 120 boards, noise 0.16 and ignores opponent reads; difficulty 3 samples 600 and 900, noise 0.04. Only difficulty 2 is verified against the recipe; 1 and 3 are new tuning, tested for behaviour (sampling, range reading), not for playing strength.
  - Verified: 400 random spots (all four streets, short and deep stacks, one to four opponents, with and without memory and styles) compared against the recipe's `botAction` with the same random sequence: identical actions.
  - Left behind: bot names (`BOT_NAMES` in `engine.ts` line 411 and `PERSONALITIES` in `bot.ts` lines 14-27), thinking time and scheduling (`BOT_MIN_MS`, `BOT_THINK_*`, `botDue`, `setActor` in `engine.ts`), rebuy acceptance by bots, and applying the action: all belong to computer-players and the engine.
